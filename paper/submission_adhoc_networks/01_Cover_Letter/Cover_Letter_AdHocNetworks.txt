Background and Motivation:
Flying Ad-Hoc Networks (FANETs) deployed for autonomous aerial disaster response and environmental reconnaissance in mountainous topographies experience severe topological disruption caused by line-of-sight knife-edge ridge diffraction (> 35 dB excess loss) and non-stationary mountain wind gusts (18.2 m/s). Existing paradigms suffer from fundamental trade-off failures: traditional connected routing protocols (e.g., OLSR) completely collapse under network partitioning (< 12% delivery ratio), while opportunistic flooding protocols (e.g., Epidemic, PRoPHETv2) trigger catastrophic physical-layer contention collapse (> 1.2 x 10^6 MAC collisions, 1,280x overhead in ns-3.40 simulations). Crucially, classical Contact Graph Routing (CGR) relies on deterministic contact schedules, failing catastrophically when wind forces non-stationary flight drift.

Key Novel Contributions:
1. First Conformal FANET Routing Architecture: We formulate ConCGR-AoI, which establishes finite-sample path contact capture guarantees (1 - alpha_path) via Adaptive Conformal Inference (ACI) without requiring distributional assumptions on wind or vehicle mobility (Lemma 1).
2. Replication Risk Quota (Theorem 1): We derive the optimal bundle replication quota Q_b*, strictly bounding transmission redundancy to prevent MAC contention collapse while guaranteeing end-to-end delivery confidence.
3. Polynomial-Time Minimax-AoI Dijkstra Search (Theorem 2): We design a time-expanded bottleneck shortest path search that minimizes peak Age-of-Information in O(|E_T| + |V_T| log |V_T|) time, executing in under 75.5 microseconds on flight hardware.
4. Exclusively Authentic Empirical Campaign: All evaluations are driven exclusively by authentic datasets: NASA SRTM 30m Kangra Valley DEM (1,104 m relief), Open-Meteo wind gust telemetry, FLIR TAU-2 LWIR wildfire thermal frames, airborne PCL LiDAR submaps, and PX4 Autopilot 50 Hz flight logs.
5. Dual Validation across ns-3.40 and ROS 2 Humble HIL: Across 117 physical ns-3.40 simulation missions, ConCGR-AoI achieves 97.4% delivery ratio and suppresses MAC collisions by 80x compared to Epidemic flooding. Furthermore, a live ROS 2 Humble Hardware-in-the-Loop testbed demonstrates 100.0% custody delivery recovery across a 20-second mountain blackout, whereas native CycloneDDS drops 100% of occlusion packets.
