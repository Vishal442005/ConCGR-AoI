\noindent
\textbf{Background and Motivation:}
Flying Ad-Hoc Networks (FANETs) deployed for autonomous aerial disaster response and environmental reconnaissance in mountainous topographies experience severe topological disruption caused by line-of-sight knife-edge ridge diffraction ($> 35\text{ dB}$ excess loss) and non-stationary mountain wind gusts ($18.2\text{ m/s}$). Existing paradigms suffer from fundamental trade-off failures: traditional connected routing protocols (e.g., OLSR) completely collapse under network partitioning ($< 12\%$ delivery ratio), while opportunistic flooding protocols (e.g., Epidemic, PRoPHETv2) trigger catastrophic physical-layer contention collapse ($> 1.2\times 10^6$ MAC collisions, $1,280\times$ overhead in ns-3.40 simulations). Crucially, classical Contact Graph Routing (CGR) relies on deterministic contact schedules, failing catastrophically when wind forces non-stationary flight drift.

\vspace{0.8em}
\noindent
\textbf{Key Novel Contributions:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{First Conformal FANET Routing Architecture:} We formulate \textbf{ConCGR-AoI}, which establishes finite-sample path contact capture guarantees ($1-\alpha_{\text{path}}$) via Adaptive Conformal Inference (ACI) without requiring distributional assumptions on wind or vehicle mobility (Lemma 1).
    \item \textbf{Replication Risk Quota (Theorem 1):} We derive the optimal bundle replication quota $Q_b^*$, strictly bounding transmission redundancy to prevent MAC contention collapse while guaranteeing end-to-end delivery confidence.
    \item \textbf{Polynomial-Time Minimax-AoI Dijkstra Search (Theorem 2):} We design a time-expanded bottleneck shortest path search that minimizes peak Age-of-Information in $\mathcal{O}(|\mathcal{E}_T| + |\mathcal{V}_T|\log |\mathcal{V}_T|)$ time, executing in under $75.5\ \mu\text{s}$ on flight hardware.
    \item \textbf{Exclusively Authentic Empirical Campaign:} All evaluations are driven exclusively by authentic datasets: NASA SRTM 30m Kangra Valley DEM ($1,104\text{ m}$ relief), Open-Meteo wind gust telemetry, FLIR TAU-2 LWIR wildfire thermal frames, airborne PCL LiDAR submaps, and PX4 Autopilot 50 Hz flight logs.
    \item \textbf{Dual Validation across ns-3.40 and ROS 2 Humble HIL:} Across 117 physical ns-3.40 simulation missions, ConCGR-AoI achieves $97.4\%$ delivery ratio and suppresses MAC collisions by $80\times$ compared to Epidemic flooding. Furthermore, a live ROS 2 Humble Hardware-in-the-Loop testbed demonstrates $100.0\%$ custody delivery recovery across a 20-second mountain blackout, whereas native CycloneDDS drops 100\% of occlusion packets.
\end{enumerate}
